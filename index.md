<div class="bio">
    <p>I am Junteng Liu (刘俊腾), a first-year PhD candidate at the <a href="https://hkust-nlp.github.io/">HKUST NLP Group</a> under the supervision of <a href="https://junxianhe.github.io/">Professor Junxian He</a>. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024.</p>

    <p>My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with particular interest in:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning</strong>: Exploring reasoning capabilities and training paradigms for large language models</li>
        <li><strong>Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)</strong>: Understanding and mitigating hallucinations in multimodal models</li>
        <li><strong>LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability</strong>: Investigating the truthfulness mechanisms and interpretability of language models</li>
    </ul>
</div>
