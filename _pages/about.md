I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group in the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. Prior to this, I completed my B.Eng. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also previously advised by Professor Junxian He and received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
