# About Me

I am Junteng Liu (刘俊腾), a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group under the supervision of [Professor Junxian He](https://junxianhe.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with particular interest in:
- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Exploring reasoning capabilities and training paradigms for large language models
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Understanding and mitigating hallucinations in multimodal models
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Investigating the truthfulness mechanisms and interpretability of language models
